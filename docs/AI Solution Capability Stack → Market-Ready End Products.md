# 4. Our Market Position

We should NOT approach the market saying:

> We sell RAG.

> We sell LangGraph.

> We sell vector databases.

> We build AI agents.

Those are implementation technologies.

The market-facing conversation should be:

> **What is taking too much time?**

> **What information is difficult to find?**

> **What work gets repeated?**

> **What is being manually copied from one place to another?**

> **What do employees repeatedly ask?**

> **What do customers repeatedly ask?**

> **What decisions require gathering information from multiple systems?**

> **What should work better?**

Then we determine whether the solution needs:

**RAG  
Agent  
Workflow  
Search  
Document Intelligence  
Data  
Automation  
MCP  
or no AI at all.**
